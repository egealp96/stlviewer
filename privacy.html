STL Viewer: 3D Print & AR – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

STL Viewer lets you open and inspect 3D model files (STL, OBJ, 3MF, PLY) on your phone or tablet. This policy explains what happens to your data.

Your 3D files stay on your device

Models you open are copied into the app's own storage on your device and are never uploaded to us or anyone else. All measurements, print estimates and AR previews are calculated on your device. There is no account and no sign-in.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may collect device identifiers (such as the advertising ID / IDFA), IP address, approximate location, app usage and ad interaction data. On iOS we only use the IDFA if you allow tracking in Apple's prompt. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent message, and you can change your choice any time in Settings › Privacy choices. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details.

Camera

On iOS the camera is used only by Apple's AR Quick Look to place your model in your room. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.
